About

Luke Fronsko

Luke started Lukey 3D to deliver high quality 3D content for screen, cultural heritage, preservation and game related industries.

Based in Darwin, NT, he is a 3D specialist and creative director with strong expertise in 3D modelling, photogrammetry, animation and digital environments.

With over five years of experience across asset creation, project management and pipeline optimisation, backed by a technical background in drone surveys, LiDAR, bathymetry and photogrammetry, Luke brings a detail oriented, technically grounded approach to every project.
